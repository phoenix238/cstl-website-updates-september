Clean Language and Craniosacral Therapy

With Phoenix Tanner, CSTA-registered therapist · Bethnal Green & Waterloo, London

Clean Language is a way to deepen the connection between mind and body, and to give voice to your body and your experience. It's part of how I work in every session. In a standard 60-minute session it comes in while you're on the table, woven through the hands-on craniosacral work.

I now also offer 90-minute sessions, so we can spend longer with Clean Language. They're available at both of my London locations.

What is Clean Language?

Clean Language is a way of asking questions that was developed by the therapist David Grove. The questions are deliberately simple, and they're built out of your own words. I don't add my assumptions, interpretations or suggestions. That's what makes them "clean".

They sound something like this:

And what kind of tightness is that tightness?

And where is that?

And is there anything else about that?

And what would you like to have happen?

Questions like these often lead people to images and metaphors: "it's like a knot", "a weight on my chest", "something wanting to open". Clean Language stays with those images and lets them become clearer and more specific, in your language rather than mine.

How I use it

For me, Clean Language is about working with your inherent healing process, mentally as well as physically. It helps us get deeper and more specific in your experience, and that supports the shifts you need to make.

I'm not here to read your mind. I'm here to help you hear yourself, to settle and be at ease, and to realise things about yourself that can be hard to find alone.

On the table, in any session, a few clean questions can help you notice what's happening in your body as it happens: where something is, what it's like, what it would like. In a 90-minute session we also have time to start with it before the hands-on work. What comes up there often gives the craniosacral work somewhere to begin, so that when you're on the table your system has already been listened to.

What happens in a 90-minute session

  • 30 minLanding and Clean LanguageSitting together, we take time to arrive. Then I ask a few simple questions using your words. You lead. There's nothing you have to talk about, and nothing to get right.
  • 60 minCraniosacral therapyYou lie fully clothed on the table while I use light, responsive touch. Clean Language keeps coming in and out as we go, so what your body says in words and what it says through touch can meet.

Is it right for me?

A 90-minute session may suit you if you'd like more time to explore what your body is holding, or you find it helps to have words for your experience as well as touch. You don't need any experience of either Clean Language or craniosacral therapy. New and returning clients are both welcome.

If you're not sure, start with a 60-minute session. Clean Language is part of that too, and you can always choose the longer session next time.

Do I have to talk about difficult things?

No. You choose what you bring, and you can always say "pass". The questions only ever use your own words, so they follow you rather than lead you somewhere you didn't choose to go.

Is this counselling or psychotherapy?

No. Clean Language here is part of a craniosacral session, a way of listening to your experience alongside the hands-on work. It isn't counselling or psychotherapy, and I won't analyse or interpret what you say.
