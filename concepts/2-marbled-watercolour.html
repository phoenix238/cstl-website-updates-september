About the practice

Let everything soften and flow

Craniosacral therapy is a gentle yet powerful hands-on approach that supports the body's natural ability to release tension, restore balance, and heal. It helps shift the nervous system into a parasympathetic state, where rest, recovery and repair happen.

Using light touch and deep presence, I create a safe space for your system to unwind and return to flow.
