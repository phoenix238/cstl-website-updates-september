# Design feedback so far

Running notes of what Phoenix likes and doesn't like across the concepts.
Read this before designing anything new for the site.

## The big picture

- **Artistic, not spiritual.** It should feel like art, not "miracle work". The Aura (5) and Spine of Light (10) concepts went too spiritual.
- **Soft and approachable over busy.** Several concepts had good ideas but too much going on (1, 3, 7) or interactions that were too hectic (8).
- **Hand-made feel.** Painterly and organic. No hard, modern or futuristic lines around images. Images should blend into the background.
- **Softer colour.** The first board's feedback was "softer, more painterly, not bold". Concept 2's colour scheme is the favourite so far.
- **Micro to macro is the big idea.** Wanted: a homepage journey from outer space → Earth → plant → cells → atoms → back out to space.

## Earlier feedback (first round, the old homepage)

- A pinned band you can't scroll past is annoying. Let people scroll freely and include a way to skip.
- Mycelium threads gave a nice "fascia" feeling.
- Too grainy or old-paper looks are out. The "looking into a microscope at an extraordinary underworld" feeling is in.
- Floating dandelion seeds are out; basic line art felt too basic.
