Ten ways the site could feel

Each concept is a short homepage with real photos and copy, and its own way of moving and responding to touch. The newest five come from the second board, about the body; the first five from the micro-to-macro board. Open one, move your mouse or finger around, then use the bar at the bottom to flip between all ten.

Board two · the bodyHeat, ink, water, nerves and light

Bolder colour and a little film grain, like the board: thermal gradients, fingerprint line drawings, figures in swirling ink, drawn nerves, and halos down the spine.

Six

Body Heat

Warm and dark, like a thermal camera, with a fine grain. Wherever your cursor or finger goes it leaves warmth that spreads and cools. The photo is shown as heat, and a lens that follows your hand reveals its real colour.

Inspired by: orange arcs, orange body, heat-map hand, sun on violet
Open concept →
Seven

Whorl

Drawn in ink. Thousands of fine lines flow like the ridges of a fingertip and curve around the photo and the words. Press anywhere and the lines give way under your finger; around the photo they swell and settle every ten seconds.

Inspired by: fingerprint hand, contour body, biro anatomy
Open concept →
Eight

Fluid Body

Real ink in water, in blue and rust, swirling round the photo. Stir it with your cursor or finger. Every eight seconds the water swells out from the photo and draws back, a slow tide. The quote sits in a turning whirlpool.

Inspired by: whirlpool figure, blue-and-rust ink silhouettes, marbled brain
Open concept →
Nine

Nerve Garden

Red and teal nerves grow out of the photo like mycelium, on soft pink, and small lights travel along them like signals. New nerves reach toward your cursor; tap and a signal travels back home.

Inspired by: drawn nervous system, body-scan arteries, glowing ribcage
Open concept →
Ten

Spine of Light

Seven soft lights run down the side of the page, from crown to sacrum. As you scroll, a light travels down them and the page warms from violet to red. "Breathe along the spine" moves the light up as you breathe in and down as you breathe out.

Inspired by: rainbow halo, chakra column, sun on violet, brain scan

Board one · micro to macroSoft, painterly and organic

The first five: pastel and quiet, from the mycelium, marbling, cells and aura pins.

One

Mycelium Silk

The smoke becomes silk: thousands of fine, hand-drawn threads, like hyphae or fascia, flowing across warm paper and over the photos in sepia and rose ink. Your cursor stirs them into slow eddies. Further down, draw a line and watch it grow roots.

Inspired by: dotted swirl, ink neurons, branching veins
Open concept →
Two

Marbled Watercolour

Soft pastel pigment floating on water. Drag through it and the colours comb and swirl behind you. Photos float in painted pools; the conditions are pastel pebbles that slowly change shape.

Inspired by: acrylic pour, marbling, ink brains
Open concept →
Three

Ripple Lines

Hand-drawn contour lines, like a map of the body or rings on water. Touch the page and a ripple travels through them; the lines wrap around each photo as if it sat on a hill. The breathing section is a pool whose rings open and settle with you.

Inspired by: contour body, op-art ripples, water, cymatics
Open concept →
Four

Soft Culture

A gentle culture of pastel cells, like looking through a warm microscope. They drift, merge and divide on their own; tap one to help it divide. The photo lives inside a cell whose membrane breathes and wobbles.

Inspired by: bacteria culture, petri dish, cells
Open concept →
Five

Aura

The quietest direction: blurred colour auras, faint roots growing along the edges, lots of air. The hero is a breathing space; press "Breathe with me" and the light breathes with you. A soft glow follows your cursor.

Inspired by: chakra aura, warm gradients, sun
