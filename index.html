About the Practice

Restorative craniosacral therapy in London, Bethnal Green & Waterloo

Craniosacral therapy is a gentle yet powerful hands-on approach that supports the body's natural ability to release tension, restore balance, and heal. It helps shift the nervous system into a parasympathetic state, where rest, recovery and repair happen. Using light touch and deep presence, I create a safe space for your system to unwind and return to flow.

How can craniosacral therapy help you? People often come when they feel stuck, physically, emotionally or both. Whether you're dealing with pain, stress, anxiety, burnout, or a general sense of disconnection, this work helps bring you back to yourself. Common concerns include migraines, jaw tension, insomnia, fatigue, trauma, and nervous system dysregulation.

How it Works

What happens in a session?

Every session is guided by what your body needs in the moment. Using a touch no heavier than the weight of a coin, I listen to the subtle rhythms that express your body's health and its places of restriction.

  1. Stage 01

    Check-in

    We begin with a short conversation about how you're arriving and what you'd like to work with.

  2. Stage 02

    On the table

    You lie fully clothed. The session unfolds in its own time, sometimes deeply still, sometimes with gentle movement.

  3. Stage 03

    Release & integration

    Your system releases what's ready to shift. Emotions and sensations are all welcome.

  4. Stage 04

    Landing

    We close gently, giving your system time to integrate before you return to the world.

Fascial Unwinding · ×100

What is fascial unwinding?

Fascia is the body's connective tissue, a continuous web that surrounds every muscle, organ, bone, and nerve. It holds structure, transmits force, and carries memory. When the body experiences stress, injury, or trauma, the fascial system holds and organises these experiences long after the event has passed.

Fascial unwinding is a gentle, responsive technique in which I support part of your body, an arm, the head, the spine, and simply follow wherever it wants to go. The body, given space and permission, naturally moves through patterns that have been held in the tissue and release them with a precision no external force could match.

The results can be remarkable: spaciousness, lightness, or profound emotional release. Sometimes physical. Often both.

What to Expect

Your session, your space

Each session begins with a short check-in. You'll lie fully clothed on the table, and I'll use gentle, responsive touch to meet your system where it's at. The experience is deeply relaxing, often leading to profound release and a renewed sense of connection.
